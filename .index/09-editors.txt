compiler/xtmd/src/lsp       the language server, with no input or output of its own

  analysis.rs   hover, definition, references, outline, completion, from the resolved IR
  text.rs       positions: the protocol's, xtmd's, bytes
  config.rs     packs from xtmd.toml
  rules.rs      each code's place in the spec (checks/rules.json)

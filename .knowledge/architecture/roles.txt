ROLES  (docs/specs/06-hosts-packs-implementers.md)
host         shows a page: holds the IR and state, gives extern values, hears extern fns
runtime      runs a page from its IR; supplies core's native fns
renderer     draws the tree on a platform, with a widget set (core's extern widgets); gives page and
             moment.local
engine       turns a page into files ahead of time
implementer  whoever provides one of these, and checks it
pack author  supplies a pack's own externs

Where state is kept, and how IR reaches a page, is the host's. Nothing of ours stores or sends it.
How well one keeps the spec is its implementer's: xtmd tests none.
"Engine" never means what runs or draws a page. Platform is the word for both together.

THE RESOLVED IR (rir)  schemas/rir.schema.json, compiler/xtmd/src/check/rir.rs
What the checker understood of a build, for tools: names resolved, values typed, nothing lowered,
prose still Markdown, every node with its span and comments. `xtmd compile --rir`.
Version 2 keeps every comment (a test counts them over the repo's files, cli_rir.rs): a node's
`comments` {doc, leading, trailing, block_start, block_end, free}; comment items {comment, span}
in lists, or a body's `comment_items` where its members are split by kind; `part_comments` for
view, markdown, tokens, uses. `block_*` say which bracket pair by `at`, counted in the parse
tree's order (the writer's walk). Front matter keeps its text. Each version a release shipped
is kept in schemas/rir-history.
What `xtmd decompile` prints back from it compiles to the same resolved IR, IR and Markdown, over
core, the cases, examples and apps (compiler/xtmd/tests/decompile.rs): so the rir keeps what the
text means where layout would say it, an empty `markdown { }`, a slot filled by the block
(`children`), a blank line beside a prose comment (it ends a paragraph). Layout it doesn't keep
is fmt's choice: where a list breaks (over 110 columns), blank lines between members.
The language server and decompile read it. Nothing that runs, draws or builds a page does.

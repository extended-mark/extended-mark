A PLANE'S LAYOUT
An engine measures each spot's child; the layout works out where spots land, the bounds, the centre
and the region, and writes them as one change a pass, at most eight passes. One that never settles
is a failure of the plane. Sizes are in the plane's units, so panning and zooming change none.
engines/published/runtime/src/layout.ts, engines/examples/rust/runtime/src/layout.rs.
Tests give fixed sizes (engines/published/runtime/test/sizes.mjs), so places are exact.

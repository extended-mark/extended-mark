A PLANE'S LAYOUT
Three parts. Pure: boxes in, places out (lay), in the plane's units, so panning and zooming change none.
The driver, with the widgets: finds planes and spots, measures, writes back (layoutPlanes on the web).
The page: settle runs passes until one changes nothing, at most eight; each pass's writes are one
change. One that never settles is a failure of what it was for, held until something else changes.
TypeScript: engines/published/runtime/src/layout.ts, engines/published/dom/src/planes.ts, settle in
engines/published/runtime/src/page.ts.
Rust: engines/examples/rust/runtime/src/layout.rs, engines/examples/rust/planes, page.rs.
Tests give fixed sizes (engines/published/runtime/test/sizes.mjs), so places are exact; the cases with
planes run with the web's widgets, engines/published/dom/test/planes.test.mjs, with no browser.

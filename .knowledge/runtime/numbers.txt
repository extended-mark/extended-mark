NUMBERS
One number type: base-10, decimal128, 34 digits, halves to even. Never a binary float.
compiler/xtmd/src/eval/num.rs (decNumber, the dec crate) for compiler and native runtime;
runtime/typescript/src/eval/num.ts for the web (decimal.js only in maths.ts, for its functions).
Values cross as decimal strings ({ "num": "3" }).
runtime/typescript/test/differential.test.mjs compares the two.

A PACK'S VERSIONS  (compiler/xtmd/src/check/versions.rs, migrations.rs)
A surface (surfaces/kit@1.xtmdpack, written by `xtmd surface`) is what a release provided. What
compiled against one release of kit@1 compiles against every later one, or it is an error.
kit@2 ships migrations/kit@2.xtmdpack, which must account for every difference from kit@1's surface.
Worked through: examples/pack-migration.
Applied (migrate/packs/) to the file's resolved IR, compiled against kit@1's surface; printed back in
fmt's layout and compiled against kit@2, which must give the IR the statements made.
A value is worked out by eval where the use gives constants; else it is the statement's code, each of
the use's values a stand-in call (`xtmd_probe_<n>()`) compiled in a probe fn, then put back as its IR.
A document's own `let` stays its name, unless only a migration-only fn could use it.
A dropped case, an execute that can't decide, a slot that isn't plain text: refused, or a TODO with
--allow-todos (exit 3). Tests: compiler/xtmd/tests/migrate_packs.rs, and compiler/xtmd/tests/cli.rs migrate_*.

KEEPING ELEMENTS
A node's element lives as long as its id: every renderer's keys follow it, so focus, the caret,
selection and what is being typed survive a change and a row's move.
Elements carry data-xtmd-site (the id), data-xtmd-widget and data-part; tests find them by these.
Angular has no virtual tree: engines/published/widgets/src/patch.ts
follows VNodes for it, as it does for the DOM.

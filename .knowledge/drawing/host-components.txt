A HOST'S OWN WIDGETS
Given by full name (`acme/chart`, `core/press`), they replace ours: `widgets` or `own` in dom's mount, a
component in a framework's XtmdDoc, XtmdPage::widget in egui, the registry on Android.
An extern nobody supplies is drawn as its Markdown and told by lacking().
A whole set may be copied as a starting point, and runs the widgets' own tests as a copy: copied.test.mjs
(engines/published/dom/test), engines/examples/rust/egui/tests/copied.rs, CopiedSetTest.kt on Android.
So a renderer takes nothing from our widgets but what any set has.
checks/packs/badge is a pack each engine supplies, for tests.

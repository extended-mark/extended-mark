WHAT THE IR TELLS BESIDE THE CODE  (compiler/lower/src: reads.rs, whole.rs, fold.rs)
reads   on each input, loop, branch, match, place, let and rule: what can be another value later. Left
        out, it reads nothing that changes. {any} means it can't be known: a fn kept in a variable.
writes  on each handler, fn and exposed fn. An event invoked writes what its handler does.
whole   on an operator: what it takes and gives are whole and fit a double exactly. From contracts of
        variables, inputs, fields, locals and declared fns' parameters; not of items or lambdas'.
Constants of plain kinds are worked out, then what they alone used is dropped (reach runs again).
What would fail is left to fail as the page runs,
and an extern fn with a default to whoever supplies it. Told last, so an IR brought from 3 is told too.
Markdown has no reads: it is written whole. Tests: compiler/check/tests/reads.rs.
